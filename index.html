New account: 30-Day Login Streak

Per, New Accounts ko phele 30-Days Login streak poori krke Apna Account Active karna hota hai. Because Phele 30-Days unki Daily Spins limit 5 spins ya 10-spins hogi.

30-days tk daily FUNToken open krke Spin karein ge (jitni 5-10 limit hogi) usse login bonus claim hojai ga. Or 30-days baad Spins limit barh jai gi, jese apki spin limit gradually barhe gi tu 2000 $FUN 4-5 din me hi reach hojai ge. Sab Accounts me.

Old Accounts vs New Users

Old Accounts ke Users ki Spins limit tu zada hai, per unke pas Spins nhi hein, bas wo log har month new Game ane ka wait krte hein or Game Play krke instantly 4000 Rs withdrawal krwa lete hein. Apne accounts ke hesaab se.

But New Users ko phele 30-Days Daily spin krke limit increase krwani hoti hai.

This is How System Works, to avoid abuse and unfair rewards.
